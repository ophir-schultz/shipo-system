### Post A — Switching 3PLs (→ /how-to-switch-3pl/)
> Most brands overpay to switch 3PLs — not on the pick fee, on the downtime.
>
> Inventory sits in limbo. Orders straddle two warehouses. You get double-billed. And if
> the new WMS mis-maps your SKUs, you find out from an angry customer.
>
> The fix is boring: get the cutover plan in writing before you sign. Who receives which
> inventory, on what date. How open orders are handled. What the rollback is.
>
> I wrote the full downtime-free switching playbook here 👇
> https://shipousa.com/how-to-switch-3pl/
>
> #3PL #ecommerce #fulfillment

### Post B — Dimensional weight (→ /dimensional-weight-calculator/)
> The fastest way to cut your shipping bill isn't a carrier discount. It's your box size.
>
> Carriers bill on the GREATER of actual vs. dimensional weight. Ship a light product in
> an oversized box and you're literally paying to ship air — often 10–20% of parcel spend.
>
> Right-size your cartons before you renegotiate anything.
>
> Free calculator to see your own numbers: https://shipousa.com/dimensional-weight-calculator/
>
> #shipping #ecommerce #logistics

### Post C — FBA prep (→ /best-3pl-amazon-fba-prep/)
> Amazon sellers pick an FBA prep partner on price per unit. Then a mislabeled FNSKU or a
> non-compliant poly bag triggers prep-service fees and a receiving hold that dwarfs the
> pennies they saved.
>
> Three questions to ask any prep center: What's your label error rate? How do you handle
> expiration/lot tracking? Can you turn receiving around in 24–48 hours during Q4?
>
> Full checklist: https://shipousa.com/best-3pl-amazon-fba-prep/
>
> #AmazonFBA #ecommerce #3PL

### Post D — What fulfillment actually costs (→ /3pl-fulfillment-cost/)

>

> watch for peak surcharges and long-term storage fees.

> I published a transparent breakdown of what it really costs:
> https://shipousa.com/3pl-fulfillment-cost/

> #3PL #ecommerce #fulfillment
