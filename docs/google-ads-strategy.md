# Shipo — Google Ads Strategy (High-Intent Search Test)

_Prepared 2026-08-11. Owner: Ophir. Status: ready to build once budget + account confirmed._

---

## 1. Objective

Capture buyers **at the moment of intent** — people actively searching for FBA prep / 3PL fulfillment — and convert them into **booked calls / quote requests**. This is a paid *demand-capture* test, not brand awareness.

**Primary conversion:** booked call or quote-request form submit.
**Secondary conversion:** phone call ≥ 60 seconds (via Google call tracking).

## 2. Budget & timeline

- **Start: $1,200/month (~$40/day).** Floor is $900/month; below that the test is too slow to read.
- **Commitment: 6 weeks** before judging. Month 1 = buying data, not ROI.
- Downside is bounded by the daily cap — Google can never exceed it.
- **Scale rule:** once an ad group produces booked calls at a cost that pencils against real per-client margin, shift budget into it and pause the rest.

> ⚠️ All CPC / conversion figures below are **industry benchmark estimates**, to be replaced with Shipo's real data after ~2 weeks live. Do not treat as actuals.

## 3. KPIs & benchmark targets (validate live)

| Metric | Starter target |
|---|---|
| CTR (high-intent search) | > 3–5% |
| Landing-page conversion rate | 2–5% |
| Est. CPC (fulfillment B2B) | $10–25 |
| Cost per lead | ~$300–500 (est.) |
| Cost per booked call | tracked from week 2 |
| Cost per **closed client** vs. margin | the real go/no-go metric |

## 4. Account structure

Three campaigns, each mapped to a real buyer intent. Separate campaigns (not just ad groups) so budget can be steered independently.

```
Campaign 1 — FBA Prep & Forwarding        (~45% of budget)
  ├─ Ad Group 1a: FBA Prep Center
  └─ Ad Group 1b: Amazon Receiving / Forwarding
Campaign 2 — 3PL / DTC Fulfillment        (~40% of budget)
  ├─ Ad Group 2a: 3PL Fulfillment
  └─ Ad Group 2b: Ecommerce / DTC Fulfillment
Campaign 3 — Delaware / No-Sales-Tax      (~15% of budget)
  └─ Ad Group 3a: Delaware Fulfillment
```

- **Networks:** Search only. **Turn OFF Display Network + Search Partners** at launch (they burn budget on low-intent junk).
- **Geo:** United States, English. Both ICPs are US-national.
- **Devices:** all, monitor mobile vs. desktop conversion and adjust.
- **Schedule:** all days at launch; add day-parting after 2 weeks of data (B2B tends to convert on weekday business hours).

## 9. Landing page (do NOT send traffic to the homepage)

A dedicated, message-matched page — ideally one strong page with sections for each intent, or 3 variants later.

**Above the fold:** headline that mirrors the search ("Amazon FBA Prep & Forwarding — Delaware, No Sales Tax"), one-line subhead, primary CTA (**Get a Quote** / **Book a Call**), short lead form (name, email, monthly order/unit volume, store platform).

**Below:** how it works (3 steps), services, trust signals (real logos/reviews only — no fabricated ones), Delaware/tax advantage, secondary CTA.

**Requirements:** mobile-fast (< 2.5s LCP), form above the fold, click-to-call on mobile, thank-you page that fires the conversion. Built on shipousa.com (WordPress/Elementor) or as a Next.js page — your call.

## 10. Conversion tracking (must be live BEFORE spend)

1. Install **Google tag (gtag)** on shipousa.com — via Site Kit, GTM, or direct.
2. Create conversion actions: **form submit** (primary), **phone call from ad** (Google forwarding number), optional **call ≥60s from website**.
3. Link **GA4** and Google Ads.
4. Fire the form-submit conversion on the thank-you page.
5. QA with Google Tag Assistant before launch. **No tracking = flying blind = don't launch.**

## 11. Bidding

- **Weeks 1–2:** Maximize Clicks **with a max-CPC ceiling** (~$20) — gather click + conversion data without overpaying.
- **After ~15–30 conversions:** switch to **Maximize Conversions**, then layer a **target CPA** once you know your real cost-per-booked-call.
- Never hand a brand-new account straight to tCPA — it has no data to optimize against.

## 12. Optimization cadence

- **Week 1:** leave bids alone (learning phase). Only fix broken tracking / obvious junk terms.
- **Weekly thereafter:** Search Terms report → add negatives; pause keywords with spend but zero conversions after a fair sample; shift budget toward ad groups producing calls; test 1 new headline per ad group.
- **Week 6:** go/no-go decision on cost-per-booked-client vs. margin.

## 13. Scaling & next layers

- Scale winning ad groups **+20–30%/week** while CPA holds.
- **Add Meta retargeting** once site traffic is flowing — cheap re-serve to Google/SEO visitors who didn't convert (this is the right time for Meta, not before).
- Expand keywords only from *proven* converting search terms.
