**Business context you'll need:**
- Compensation has **two parts**, and a partner gets both: a **one-time $300 bonus** per
  referred client, **plus 5% of the net profit** on that client's account, paid monthly for
  **12 months** from the client's first paid invoice (§3.3 and §3.4). "Net profit" is
  defined in §3.5 as the month's invoiced amount less the direct costs of serving the
  account; warehouse labor is not deducted.
- Both are owed once the referred client signs and pays its **first invoice of any size**
  (§3.1). There is **no minimum invoice amount and no waiting period** — see the next
  point, which is the main thing I want your view on.
- Partners are independent contractors; we'll 1099 them.
- We're a Delaware LLC and want Delaware governing law.
- Partners will sign via DocuSign.

**Specific points I'd like you to confirm or fix:**

1. **Commission structure (§3)** — Is the $300 + 5%/12-month structure clean and
   enforceable? Any issue with the "Qualified Referral" definition in §3.1?
2. **Clawback (§3.9) — please start here.** This draft previously gated the $300 on the
   client being billed $500+ in a month and on 60 days of activity. I removed **both** on
   2026-10-12 (see the attorney note at §3.1/§3.2), knowing the exposure: the bonus is now
   payable on a client that signs, pays one small invoice, and leaves, and a small account
   may never return the $300 out of the 5% share. **§3.9 is now the only protection against
   that**, and it is unfinished — I need you to choose the mechanism (refund vs. offset
   against future amounts) and the cure period. In particular: is an **offset-only** remedy
   worth anything against a partner who refers once and never refers again, there being
   nothing further to offset against? If a minimum-term or repayment covenant is the answer,
   please draft it into §3.9.
3. **1099 / W-9 (§4)** — Is our independent-contractor language and W-9-before-payment gate
   correct for Delaware / federal tax purposes?
4. **Anti-spam / conduct (§5)** — Is the CAN-SPAM and "no guarantees / no tax-benefit
   claims" language sufficient to protect us if a partner oversells? (Note: our Delaware
   "0% sales tax" is a *location* fact — partners must not claim it lowers a client's own
   sales-tax obligation.)
5. **Liability cap & indemnification (§8–§9)** — Is the cap reasonable and are the
   carve-outs right? Should indemnification be one-way (partner→Shipo) or mutual?
6. **Dispute forum (§10)** — Recommend: Delaware courts vs. binding arbitration, and venue.
7. **Anything missing** — Non-solicitation, IP/trademark use, data protection, or other
