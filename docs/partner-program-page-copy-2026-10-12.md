# Partner Program page — proposed copy change

**Page:** https://shipousa.com/partner-program/ (WordPress post 2072, Elementor)
**Form:** Forminator 2074 (the partner-type dropdown)
**Status:** DRAFT — **not written to the live site.** Awaiting Ophir's approval.
**Date:** 2026-10-12

Two changes in one pass, because they touch the same blocks:

1. **Remove both gates on the $300**, matching the agreement change committed today
   (`9c9eb9b`). The page currently publishes `bills ≥ $500 in a calendar month` and
   `60 days active`. Both are gone from the code and from the agreement, so the page is
   now the only place that still asserts them — and it is the place a partner reads
   before signing.
2. **Open the program to DTC**, not just FBA prep. Nearly every line of the page says
   "prep", "Amazon", or "FBA".

## The page already contradicts itself

The video caption at the foot of the page reads:

> Refer a brand that needs **DTC fulfillment or Amazon FBA prep**, and earn when they
> ship with Shipo.

So the DTC half of the program is already promised on this page, in one line, under a
page whose headline, subhead, hero paragraph and every benefit bullet say FBA prep only.
Change 2 is not a new claim — it is making the rest of the page agree with a sentence
already on it.

Everything below is DTC that Shipo already sells and already has pages for: Fulfillment
Center, Worldwide Parcel Shipping, Beauty & Cosmetics Fulfillment, Supplement
Fulfillment. **No new service claim and no new number is introduced anywhere in this
draft.**

## Part 1 — the two gates

### 1a. Terms summary, "$300 referral bounty" (the legally material one)

This block is the page's public summary of the agreement. It has to match clauses 3.1,
3.2 and 3.10 as committed today.

**NOW:**

> **$300 referral bounty:** paid once per referred client, after that client becomes an
> active, qualifying account — **bills ≥ $500 in a calendar month, 60 days active,** and
> first invoice paid in full. The referred seller must be new to Shipo and not already in
> contact with us at the time of your introduction. Register every referral in writing —
> using the form on this page or by email to Support@shipousa.com — *before* the prospect
> contacts us; an introduction we only hear about afterwards does not qualify. We may
> decline any referral.

**PROPOSED:**

> **$300 referral bounty:** paid once per referred client, after that client signs with
> Shipo and pays their first invoice in full. **There is no minimum invoice amount and no
> waiting period** — an invoice of any size qualifies once it is paid, and nothing further
> needs to elapse. The referred brand must be new to Shipo and not already in contact with
> us at the time of your introduction. Register every referral in writing — using the form
> on this page or by email to Support@shipousa.com — *before* the prospect contacts us; an
> introduction we only hear about afterwards does not qualify. We may decline any referral.

Three things kept deliberately, because they are still in the agreement and still
protect Shipo: **first invoice paid in full** (3.1, limb (b) — an invoice that issues but
is not paid does not qualify), **new to Shipo / not already in contact**, and **register
in writing before contact**. Only the two numeric gates come out.

The absence is stated **positively** ("no minimum invoice amount and no waiting period")
rather than by deleting the words. A page merely silent on thresholds gives a partner no
answer when the question comes up, and it is the same reason clauses 3.2 and 3.10 were
repurposed instead of deleted.

### 1b. Hero, "$300 for every client you refer"

**NOW:** "Introduce a seller who needs prep — we quote, onboard, and handle everything.
Once they're an **active, qualifying account,** you get $300."

**PROPOSED:** "Introduce a brand — we quote, onboard, and handle everything. **Once they
sign and pay their first invoice, you get $300** — whatever the invoice is worth."

"Active, qualifying account" was the phrase the $500/60-day gates defined. With the gates
gone it is a term with nothing behind it, which reads as a reserved discretion a partner
cannot check.

### 1c. "How it works" step 4

**NOW:** "**You earn** — Client goes active — you get $300, then 5% of profit every month
for the next 12 months."

**PROPOSED:** "**You earn** — Client pays their first invoice — you get $300, then 5% of
profit every month for the next 12 months."

"Goes active" is the same undefined term as above.

## Part 2 — DTC

### 2a. Headline and subhead

**NOW:**
> # Shipo Partner Program
> ## Refer sellers who need prep. Then earn 5% every month for a year.
> Send us Amazon sellers who need reliable US FBA prep & forwarding — we quote, onboard,
> and do all the work. You earn on every account you bring.

**PROPOSED:**
> # Shipo Partner Program
> ## Refer brands that need fulfillment. Then earn 5% every month for a year.
> Send us brands that need DTC fulfillment or Amazon FBA prep — we quote, onboard, and do
> all the work. You earn on every account you bring.

"Sellers" → "brands" throughout: "seller" is Amazon's word and quietly re-narrows the
page to Amazon even where "prep" has been removed.

### 2b. Hero paragraphs

**NOW:**
> Shipo is a Delaware hub that receives, preps, stores, and forwards Amazon FBA inventory
> — all under one roof.
>
> If you work with product brands, importers, or Amazon sellers, they need dependable US
> prep. Introduce them to us and earn on every account, while we handle receiving, prep,
> storage, and forwarding into Amazon.

**PROPOSED:**
> Shipo is a Delaware hub that receives, stores, picks, packs and ships — direct to your
> client's customers, or prepped and forwarded into Amazon FBA. All under one roof.
>
> If you work with product brands, importers, or Amazon sellers, they need dependable US
> fulfillment. Introduce them to us and earn on every account, while we handle receiving,
> storage, pick & pack, DTC shipping, FBA prep and forwarding.

### 2c. Benefit bullets — two of six change

The other four ("0% Delaware sales tax", "1–2 day Northeast delivery", "No-surprise
pricing", "Founder-led, same-day replies") are service-neutral already and are left
**verbatim**. No rate or delivery claim is touched.

| | NOW | PROPOSED |
|---|---|---|
| Storage bullet | **Storage + forwarding built in** — Buffer stock at the hub, forward into Amazon on demand. | **Storage + shipping built in** — Buffer stock at the hub. Ship to customers or forward into Amazon on demand. |
| Zero-ops bullet | **Zero ops burden on you** — We quote, onboard and serve — you just make the intro. | *(unchanged)* |

Also in the "0% Delaware sales tax" bullet: "Inventory lands, is stored, prepped &
forwarded with nothing added on top." → "Inventory lands, is stored, picked, packed and
shipped with nothing added on top." Same claim, both service lines.

⚠️ The "0% Delaware sales tax" claim itself is left exactly as written. Per the attorney
cover note it is a **location** fact and partners must not pitch it as lowering a
client's own sales-tax obligation — I am not touching that wording.

### 2d. "Two ways to partner" — Referral Partner column

**NOW:** Best for — "Agencies, consultants, freight forwarders, sourcing agents"

**PROPOSED:** Best for — "Ecommerce and DTC agencies, Shopify developers, consultants,
freight forwarders, sourcing agents"

The White-Label column and the 15% wholesale margin are **unchanged** — I have no
instruction on that half and no verified basis to restate it.

### 2e. Form 2074 partner-type dropdown

**NOW:** Amazon agency / account manager · Freight forwarder / customs broker · Sourcing
agent · Consultant / coach · Aggregator / brand holdco · Other

**PROPOSED — add three, keep all six, keep "Other" last:**

- Ecommerce / DTC agency
- Shopify / web developer
- Brand consultant (DTC)

Keeping the existing six matters: `/api/referrals/intake` stores whatever the form sends,
so renaming an existing option orphans the value on every row already recorded under it.

## What I did NOT change, and why

- **Any number other than the two removed gates.** $300, 5%, 12 months, $50 carry-forward,
  30-day payment, 15% wholesale, "1–2 day Northeast", "$0 setup / $0 SKU onboarding",
  "Freight at actual cost" — all left verbatim. The homepage table is the only
  authoritative rate card and this page is not it.
- **The "5% for 12 months", "Payment" and "General" terms paragraphs.** Nothing in
  today's change touches them, and they already match the agreement. The DTC opening does
  not alter them: the 5% is on net profit of whatever was invoiced and never cared which
  service line it came from.
- **The White-Label track.**
- **The SEO title** ("3PL Referral Program — Earn $300 + 5% for 12 Months | Shipo"), which
  is service-neutral and carries no gate.

## Open question for you

**The two gates are live on the page right now.** Until it is edited, a partner can read
`≥ $500 in a calendar month` and `60 days active` on shipousa.com while the agreement
they would sign says there is no minimum and no waiting period. That gap favours the
partner — the page is stricter than the contract, so nobody is underpaid by it — but it
is a published term that disagrees with a signed one, and it is the page a partner is sent
to. Worth doing in the same sitting as the agreement.
