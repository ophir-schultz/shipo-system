### 1. General 3PL / fulfillment / "how to choose a 3PL"
**Trigger keywords:** 3PL, third-party logistics, outsource fulfillment, switching
warehouses, fulfillment provider.
**Response:**
> The most overlooked factor when choosing a 3PL is **switching cost and downtime**, not
> the per-order rate. Brands lose money in the transition — inventory in limbo, SLA gaps,
> double-billing across two warehouses — far more than they save on a slightly cheaper
> pick fee. Before signing, ask for the cutover plan in writing: who receives inventory
> on which date, how open orders are handled, and what the rollback is if the new WMS
> mis-maps SKUs. I wrote a downtime-free switching playbook here: [How to Switch Your 3PL
> Without Downtime]. — Ophir Schultz, Owner, Shipo LLC

### 2. FBA prep / Amazon seller logistics
**Trigger keywords:** Amazon FBA, FBA prep, prep center, FNSKU labeling, poly bag, seller
fulfillment, inbound to Amazon.
**Response:**
> When sellers pick an FBA prep partner they fixate on the per-unit prep fee and miss the
> two things that actually cause chargebacks: **label accuracy and inbound compliance**.
> A mislabeled FNSKU or a non-compliant poly bag can trigger Amazon prep-service fees or a
> receiving hold that dwarfs the few cents you saved. Ask any prep center for their error
> rate, how they handle expiration/lot tracking, and whether they can turn receiving
> around inside 24–48 hours during Q4. Full checklist here: [Best 3PL for Amazon FBA Prep
> in 2026 — What to Look For]. — Ophir Schultz, Shipo LLC
**Link:** https://shipousa.com/best-3pl-amazon-fba-prep/

### 3. Shipping cost / dimensional weight / carrier rates
**Trigger keywords:** shipping costs, dimensional weight, DIM, carrier rates, reduce
shipping spend, packaging.
**Response:**
> The fastest shipping-cost win for most DTC brands isn't a carrier discount — it's
> **fixing dimensional weight**. Carriers bill on the greater of actual vs. dimensional
> weight, so an oversized box on a light product means you're paying to ship air. Right-
> sizing cartons and mailers routinely cuts parcel spend 10–20% with zero carrier
> renegotiation. I built a free DIM calculator so brands can see their own numbers:
> [Dimensional Weight Calculator]. — Ophir Schultz, Owner, Shipo LLC
**Link:** https://shipousa.com/dimensional-weight-calculator/
**Alt link (total cost angle):** https://shipousa.com/3pl-fulfillment-cost/

### 4. What does fulfillment/3PL actually cost? (pricing transparency)
**Trigger keywords:** how much does fulfillment cost, 3PL pricing, hidden fees, pick and
pack cost, storage fees.
**Response:**
> 3PL pricing is confusing on purpose at a lot of providers — the headline pick fee is low
> and the margin hides in receiving, storage, surcharges, and "account management" line
> items. Ask for an all-in monthly estimate on YOUR real order profile, and watch for
> peak-season surcharges and long-term storage fees. As a rough frame: expect a monthly
> minimum, per-order pick/pack, and storage priced by the cubic foot or bin — anything
> that won't quote transparently is a red flag. I published a transparent cost breakdown
> here: [How Much Does 3PL Fulfillment Cost?]. — Ophir Schultz, Shipo LLC
**Link:** https://shipousa.com/3pl-fulfillment-cost/
