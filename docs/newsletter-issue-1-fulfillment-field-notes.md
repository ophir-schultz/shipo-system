### This month: what 3PL fulfillment actually costs

Most 3PL quotes lead with a low per-order pick fee — because that's the number that looks
good in a sales deck. The margin usually hides somewhere else: receiving, storage,
peak-season surcharges, and "account management" line items.

Before you sign with anyone (us included), ask for an **all-in monthly estimate on your
real order profile** — your actual SKUs, order volume, and seasonality — not a generic
per-unit rate. Then check for the two costs that quietly compound: **peak-season
surcharges** and **long-term storage fees**.

We wrote a transparent, line-by-line breakdown of what fulfillment really costs — what to
expect, and what a red flag looks like:

**→ How Much Does 3PL Fulfillment Cost?**
https://shipousa.com/3pl-fulfillment-cost/

### Try this: see your own dimensional-weight number

One of the fastest ways to cut a shipping bill isn't a carrier discount — it's box size.
Carriers bill on the *greater* of actual vs. dimensional weight, so a light product in an
oversized box means you're paying to ship air. Run your own cartons through our free
calculator and see the gap:

**→ Dimensional Weight Calculator**
https://shipousa.com/dimensional-weight-calculator/

### Field tip of the month

Pull last year's Q4 order data and forecast your top SKUs **by week, not by month**. Peak
demand is spiky and concentrated — a monthly average hides the days you'll actually stock
out. Buffer your winners; a stockout during peak costs far more than a little extra storage.
